Our mission

Notice the world around you

Most of us walk past hundreds of plants, mushrooms and clouds every day without really seeing them. We make small apps that turn the moments you reach for your phone into lessons about nature, so you start to see it again.

We stopped looking

Our eyes are on our screens, and the living world fades into the background: the tree on your street, the mushrooms after the rain, the clouds that bring a storm.

One name changes everything

Once you know a name, you start to see it everywhere. A walk to the shop becomes a little adventure, and a minute on your phone becomes a minute well spent.

We protect what we love

People protect what they love, and love what they know. Every name you learn is a reason to slow down and appreciate the place you live a little more.

Why we need your help
