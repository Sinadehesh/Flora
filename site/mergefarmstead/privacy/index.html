Merge Farmstead privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Merge Farmstead has no accounts and no analytics, and your game is saved only on your phone; we never receive it. The Android app shows video ads only when you choose to watch one, from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

This policy covers Merge Farmstead, a merge game with a mystery, for Android (package com.sinadehesh.mergefarmstead) and its web version. “We” means Merge Farmstead’s developer, the developer named on Merge Farmstead’s Google Play page. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

What the game accesses on your phone

Merge Farmstead asks for no special permissions. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it has no sign-in. It talks to two outside services: Google Play, when you make a purchase, and Google AdMob, for the optional video ads (see “Ads” below).

What is stored, where, and for how long

The game saves these in its private storage on your phone (in the web version, in your browser’s storage for the game’s site):

  • Your game: your farm and boards, items, coins, gems and energy, story progress, and settings such as sound.
  • What you bought, so each purchase is given to you once.
  • Shop details: which offers you have seen and taken and how long you have played, plus a rough guess of your region (from your phone’s time zone and language setting) and of how powerful your phone is (from its model name, memory and number of processor cores). The game uses these only on your phone, to choose which offers to show you. No location permission is used.

We never receive any of it. It stays until you delete it: uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings (or clearing the site’s data in your browser) erases everything.

What is collected and shared

We, the developer, do not receive any data about you: the game sends neither your game nor anything about you to us, and we do not sell data. The game contains no analytics. The one third-party code that collects data is Google’s AdMob, described next.

Purchases

Gems, packs and the Golden Pass are optional one-time purchases sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment under Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); Merge Farmstead never sees your card or payment details. When you buy something, Google Play tells the game on your phone which item you bought and gives an order reference, which the game uses only to give you the item once.

Ads (Google AdMob)

In the Android app you can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward, such as energy or gems. Ads never play unless you tap a “Watch an ad” button. They are provided by Google AdMob, which runs inside the app and, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can give a rough idea of where you are (your region, not your exact location);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as how the app and ads perform.

Google sends this data encrypted and handles it under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Ads may be personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the game’s ⚙️ Settings (“Ad privacy choices”). Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings (Google > Ads, or Security & privacy > Ads). Ads are limited to those suitable for teens. The web version shows no real ads.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the game; see GitHub’s privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The game itself keeps your progress in your browser and sends nothing to us.

Security

Your game data never leaves your phone and is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read Merge Farmstead’s private storage. The ad data Google collects is sent encrypted.

Your choices

You can stop playing, skip any offer or ad, or uninstall the game at any time, and change your ad choices as described under “Ads”. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request; for data Google holds, use Google’s tools at https://myaccount.google.com. Your game is only on your phone, so if it is deleted we cannot bring it back.

Children

Merge Farmstead is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Questions about privacy: use the contact email on Merge Farmstead’s Google Play listing, or open an issue at https://github.com/Sinadehesh/animal-farm-merge/issues.
