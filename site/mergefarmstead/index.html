A merge game with a mystery. Merge a seed pouch all the way up to a golden granary, fix up an old farm and the town of Sunnyvale, and follow the clues someone left just for you.

The story

For a hundred years the Golden Bell rang over Sunnyvale every night to call everyone home. The night of the storm, it was gone, and the families left. Now Mr. Grimsby wants the whole valley for a parking lot, and he has until the Harvest Festival.

You are the new farmer. In the planks of the old barn you find a note. Someone in town knows what happened, and they are counting on you.

How you play

Merge

Put two of the same together to make something better: eggs, hay, flowers, fish, tools and more.

Fix up the farm

Finish jobs to mend the barn, the fields and the pond, then bring the town of Sunnyvale back to life.

Solve the mystery

Find notes and clues, meet the town, and guess who the secret friend is before the last chapter.
