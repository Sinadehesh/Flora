How FloraLock works

An app blocker that doesn’t just say “no”. Every time you open a distracting app, you spend five seconds learning something real.

Pick the apps that eat your time

Choose any Android app to lock: social media, video, games, shopping. Set how many new plants you want to learn a day.

Learn a few plants every day

Each lesson shows a handful of plants once, with real photos, how to recognise them and what they’re mistaken for, then a quick quiz.

Name the plant to get in

Open a locked app and FloraLock asks about one of your plants. Right answer: the app opens for 10 minutes. Wrong: a short pause to learn its name.

Built to make it stick

Everything in FloraLock is designed around one idea: the few seconds before you scroll are perfect for learning.

Questions

What is FloraLock?

FloraLock is an app locker for Android. You choose the apps that eat your time; when you open one, FloraLock shows a plant photo with four names. Name it correctly and the app opens for 10 minutes.

Is FloraLock free?

Yes. The free version locks up to 2 apps and teaches 35 flowers. FloraLock Plus is an optional one-time purchase that adds unlimited apps, houseplants and trees. There are no ads and no subscription.

Can I still use my apps in an emergency?

Yes. You get a few emergency unlocks every day (2 by default), and you can always choose “I don’t need this app right now”.

Does FloraLock collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no analytics and no ads. FloraLock works offline. Read the privacy policy.

Which phones does FloraLock work on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or later. An iPhone version is planned.
