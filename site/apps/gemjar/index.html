Questions

Is Gem Jar free?

Yes, it is free to play, with ads. An optional one-time “No Ads” purchase switches off the banner and the ads between games; the videos you can choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game plays without internet; only the ads need a connection.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7 and up. You can also play it right now in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your game is saved on your phone, so you can pick up right where you left off.

What data does it collect?

The game itself sends nothing about you to us. The ads come from Google AdMob; the privacy policy explains what it collects and your choices.
