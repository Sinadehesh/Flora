Questions

What is CloudLock?

CloudLock is an app locker for Android. When you open an app you locked, it shows a cloud photo with four names, usually including its real look-alikes. Name it correctly and the app opens for 10 minutes.

Which clouds will I learn?

36 clouds from the WMO International Cloud Atlas: the 10 main types (cumulus, stratus, cirrus, cumulonimbus and the rest), their best-known species such as lenticular and towering cumulus, and special clouds like mammatus, shelf clouds, noctilucent clouds and fallstreak holes.

Can CloudLock forecast the weather?

No. It teaches what clouds usually mean, which is useful and fun, but only your local weather service can warn you about storms. In stormy weather, take shelter.

Is CloudLock free?

Yes. The free version locks up to 2 apps and teaches the 10 main cloud types. CloudLock Plus is an optional one-time purchase that adds unlimited apps and 26 more clouds. No ads, no subscription.

Does CloudLock collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no analytics and no ads. It works offline. Read the privacy policy.
