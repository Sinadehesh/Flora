Learn the look-alikes that matter

In mycology, the hard part is telling twins apart. ShroomLock deals real look-alikes as the wrong answers, and explains the difference when you fall for one.

ChanterellevsJack-o’-lantern

Blunt, forked ridges and grows from soil; the jack-o’-lantern has true gills and grows in clusters on wood.

Field mushroomvsDestroying angel

Pink gills turning brown; the destroying angel’s gills stay white and its base sits in a cup.

MorelvsFalse morel

Cut it top to bottom: a morel is one hollow chamber; a false morel is cottony and folded like a brain.

Honey fungusvsFuneral bell

A white spore print and cottony ring; the deadly funeral bell drops rusty-brown spores.

Questions

What is ShroomLock?

ShroomLock is an app locker for Android. When you open an app you locked, it shows a mushroom photo with four names, usually including its real look-alikes. Name it correctly and the app opens for 10 minutes.

Can I use ShroomLock to decide which mushrooms are safe to eat?

No. ShroomLock teaches recognition, not foraging. Photos can’t show smell, spore print or what grows underground, and some deadly mushrooms look like edible ones. Never eat a wild mushroom because of an app; have every find checked in person by an expert.

Is ShroomLock free?

Yes. The free version locks up to 2 apps and teaches 35 gilled mushrooms. ShroomLock Plus is an optional one-time purchase that adds unlimited apps and 36 more fungi: boletes, brackets, chanterelles, morels, puffballs and more. No ads, no subscription.

Does ShroomLock collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no analytics and no ads. It works offline. Read the privacy policy.

How is ShroomLock different from FloraLock?

They share the same app lock. ShroomLock teaches mushrooms instead of plants, and puts extra weight on dangerous look-alikes: deadly species are marked everywhere, and picking a look-alike explains how to tell the two apart.
