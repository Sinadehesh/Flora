Help us launch three app lockers

FloraLock, ShroomLock and CloudLock lock your distracting apps until you name a plant, a mushroom or a cloud. Before they can go public, Google needs real people to test them for 14 days. It’s free: send us your Gmail address first, and once we’ve added you (usually within a day) you can install the apps.

How to join

Start by sending us your Gmail address. You can’t join the test or install the app until we’ve added your address, so steps 2 and 3 only work after we reply on WhatsApp that you’re in.

  1. Send us your Gmail

    Join our WhatsApp group and send us the Gmail address you use on Google Play. We add it to the test, usually within a day, and reply when you’re in. One group covers all three apps.

  2. After we reply: become a tester

    Only once we’ve told you you’re in: pick an app below and tap “Become a tester” on its Google Play test page, with that account.

  3. Then install and use it

    Install it from Google Play, keep it for 14 days and answer a quiz now and then.

What testers do

  • Keep the app for 14 days. Uninstalling or leaving the test before then means it doesn’t count.
  • Set it up once: choose how many to learn a day, lock one or two apps you use (Instagram, YouTube…) and allow the two permissions it asks for.
  • Every day or two: open a locked app and answer the quiz, and do the one-minute daily lesson.
  • Tell us what you notice: crashes, a lock that didn’t appear or confusing text. Use “Send feedback” on the app’s Google Play page.

Tester questions

Which Google account should I use?

The one signed in to Google Play on your Android phone. Send us that Gmail address, and use the same account for the test page and the install.

The test page says I can’t join yet.

We haven’t added your email yet. We add new testers by hand, usually within a day; check you sent the Gmail you use on Google Play, then try again.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. Testing is free, and you don’t need to buy Plus. The apps have no ads and don’t collect your data.

Can I stop testing?

Yes, any time: tap “Leave the program” on the test page. If you can, please stay for the 14 days; that’s what Google counts.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The closed test is for Android phones (Android 8 or later).
