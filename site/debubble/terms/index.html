What DeBubble is, and what it is not

DeBubble offers small self-care routines and gradual steps outside your comfort zone, drawn from ideas used in habit research and in cognitive behavioural approaches. It is a self-help tool. It is not therapy, medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a crisis or emergency service. If you are struggling, or think you may have a mental or physical health condition, please talk to a qualified professional. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line straight away.

Staying safe on missions

Missions are suggestions, such as walking a new street or saying hello to someone. You decide whether, when and how to do each one. Use your own judgement: only do what is safe and legal where you are, respect other people and private property, and skip, swap or change any mission that doesn’t suit your health, your situation or your surroundings. You are responsible for your own safety and actions while doing a mission.
