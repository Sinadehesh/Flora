DeBubble privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

DeBubble has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not even have permission to use the internet. Everything you put into it stays on your phone; we never receive any of it.

Who we are

This policy covers the DeBubble app for Android (package com.debubble.app), a self-care app: small daily tasks for your body, your room and your heart, and small steps outside your comfort zone, shown as a pixel-art room that grows as you go. “We” means DeBubble’s developer, the developer named on DeBubble’s Google Play page. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

What DeBubble accesses on your phone, and why

DeBubble asks for only these permissions. Each one is used only on your phone, only for the reason given, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings.

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set yourself. DeBubble asks the first time you set a reminder.
  • Run at start-up: so the reminders you set still go off after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for a small buzz when you finish something.

DeBubble does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, has no sign-in, and does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What is stored, where, and for how long

To work, DeBubble keeps what you give it, in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your answers when you set up the app, such as how you usually get around, how far from home you tend to go, how much you want to spend on activities, and the habits you said you would like to change.
  • The routines you choose and the moments you tie them to (for example, “when I put the kettle on”), your check-ins on how your body, room and heart are doing, and how a task felt afterwards.
  • The tasks and missions you have done or tried, what you noted when one did not go to plan, your guesses before a mission and how it actually went.
  • Your journal notes, the reminder times you set, your character’s look, the items in your room, your progress and your settings.

Some of this is personal, and some of it is about how you feel, which is why none of it leaves your phone through the app. We never receive it. It stays until you delete it: you can delete a journal note in the app, and uninstalling DeBubble or clearing its storage in Android settings erases everything.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include DeBubble’s data in your device backup in your Google account, and copy it to a new phone when you switch, so you keep your progress. That backup is made and kept by Google under Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

What is collected and shared

DeBubble does not collect personal or sensitive data. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else, does not sell data, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code.

When you install DeBubble from Google Play, Google handles that under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with app developers, Google may show us statistics such as install counts, and technical crash reports; these never include anything you wrote or recorded in DeBubble.

Your home screen

If you add the DeBubble widget, it shows your room and today’s tasks on your home screen, and reminders appear as notifications. Anyone who can see your screen may see them. You can remove the widget, and choose what notifications show on your lock screen, in Android settings.

Security

Because your data never leaves your phone, it is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read DeBubble’s private storage. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so a screen lock is a good idea.

Your choices

You can skip any task, turn off reminders or revoke any permission in Android settings, delete journal notes, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. Your progress is only on your phone (and in your Google backup, if you use it), so if it is deleted we cannot bring it back.

Children

DeBubble is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If DeBubble ever starts handling information differently, for example if purchases are added through Google Play, this policy will be updated first, and the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Questions about privacy: use the contact email on DeBubble’s Google Play listing, or open an issue at https://github.com/Sinadehesh/Debubble/issues.
