How DeBubble works

A few minutes a day. Nothing to keep up with, nothing to lose.

  1. Four tiny things

    Each day, one thing for your body, your room and your heart (like washing up, clearing one surface or naming how you feel) and one small mission outside. Each takes a few minutes, and you choose the routines.

  2. Your room grows

    You start with an empty room. Looking after yourself earns the lamp, the plant, the cat. Skip a few days and it gets a little messy, but nothing you earned is ever taken away.

  3. Step outside

    Missions arrive as letters: a new street, a new café, a hello. They start tiny and grow with you, and every one you do opens the room a bit more: a window, a door, the street.

Gentle on purpose

Remind me, right there

Next to “I did it” on every task: pick this evening, before bed or any time, and get one nudge. Or none.

No streaks to lose

DeBubble counts the days you showed up, and that number only ever goes up. A missed day is just a day.

Guess, then go

Before a mission, write what you think will happen. Afterwards, see how it really went. It is usually easier than you thought.

A spark, not a fail

Didn’t go to plan? Say what got in the way and it becomes a spark of courage in your jar.

Journeys

Thirty stops toward something you want, like a real circle of friends or a life worth describing.

On your home screen

A widget shows your room and today’s four, so the next tiny step is always one tap away.

See it in action

The real app, three weeks in: a furnished room, a journey under way and a reminder set for this evening.

Built on how habits stick

Every part of DeBubble comes from what research says helps new habits last and makes brave things feel smaller.

Tied to a moment you already have

Each routine is linked to a cue you choose, like “when I put the kettle on”. If-then plans like this make people more likely to follow through.

Tiny first, bigger later

Missions climb a ladder one small rung at a time, the way people build confidence in situations that feel hard.

Checking the forecast

Writing down what you fear will happen, then comparing it with what did, is how worries lose their grip.

Rewards you can see

A room that fills up and a character you dressed yourself make progress visible, which keeps people coming back.

Questions

Is DeBubble free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, with no ads.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. DeBubble has no account and doesn’t even have permission to use the internet. Everything you write stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing you earned is taken away and there is no streak to break. Your room gets a little messy if you skip a few days, and tidies up again as soon as you’re back.

Do I have to go outside every day?

No. The mission outside is one of four, and you can swap it, save it for later, or say what got in the way. The other three happen at home.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. DeBubble is for Android phones (Android 8 or later).
