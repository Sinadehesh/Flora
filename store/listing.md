## Full description (2238/4000)

```text
FloraLock is an app blocker that teaches you something. Turn every urge to scroll into a five-second botany lesson.

Pick the apps that eat your time, such as social media, video or games. When you open one, FloraLock first shows a photo of a plant you have learned, with four names to choose from.

HOW IT WORKS
• Right answer: the app opens for the time you chose (10 minutes by default).
• Wrong answer: a short pause shows the right name and how to tell the two apart, so tapping at random doesn't pay off.
• Changed your mind? "I don't need this app right now" is always one tap away, and a few emergency unlocks a day are there when you really need one.

LEARN A FEW PLANTS A DAY
• A short daily lesson introduces new plants, then a quick exam checks them.
• Spaced review brings each plant back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days until you have mastered it.
• Wrong answers are real look-alikes, such as peony vs rose, bluebell vs hyacinth, lilac vs lavender, so you learn the differences that matter.
• Field clues on every card: flower, leaves, season and the one feature that gives it away, plus whether it is edible or toxic.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 70 plants: 35 flowers, plus houseplants and trees, each with three real photos.
• A daily streak, a collection with milestones, and your Botany IQ.
• Light and dark mode.

FREE, WITH AN OPTIONAL UPGRADE
• Free: Lock up to 2 apps and learn all 35 flowers.
• FloraLock Plus: Unlimited locked apps and all 70 plants, including houseplants and trees. Buy it once, or pay monthly and cancel anytime.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

PERMISSIONS
Locking apps is the core feature of FloraLock. To do it, it asks for:
• Usage access, to see which app is in front.
• Display over other apps, to show the question over a locked app.
• A notification, so Android keeps the lock running.
This information never leaves your phone.

SAFETY
FloraLock teaches recognition. Never eat or use a wild plant because an app said it is safe.

Photos come from iNaturalist under Creative Commons licences; every photo is credited in the app.

Website: https://www.sinadehesh.com/floralock/
Privacy policy: https://www.sinadehesh.com/floralock/privacy/
```
